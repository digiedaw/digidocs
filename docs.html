DMW — NEW PLAYER & JUMPING / NOVICE/ PROGRESSION GUIDE​

From a Fresh Tamer to Your First Proper Build Welcome to DMW! Starting on DMW can feel overwhelming because there are many systems, Digimon, questlines, dungeons and equipment upgrades available. This guide is designed to give new players a clear progression path:

Create Tamer → Novice/ Jumping → Choose Your Role → Build Your Digimon → Unlock Maps → Improve

Gear → Complete Daily Content → Begin Dungeon Progression You do not need to complete everything immediately. DMW progression is much easier when you work toward one goal at a time.

Why choosing a role matters. Do not blindly upgrade every piece of equipment you receive.
Your chosen role affects:
Digimon → Accessory Stats → Vice → Blessing → Gear Progression The Super Jumping system helps considerably because it provides equipment and Digimon options thatallow a new player to begin building toward these roles. If you are unsure which role to play, ask experienced players before spending valuable resources or making an important ticket selection.

Use the sidebar to start exploring.

UNDERSTANDING DMW ROLES​

One of the most important differences a new player should understand is that Digimon in DMW can fill different combat roles. The three main roles you should know are:

Auto Attack DPS​

AA builds focus primarily on dealing damage through normal attacks. Your Digimon choice, accessories, Vice and other equipment should eventually be optimized around AA performance.

Skill DPS​

SK builds focus primarily on dealing damage through Digimon skills. SK players therefore use a different equipment setup from AA players as they progress.

Tank​

Tank builds focus on surviving encounters and handling the defensive side of party content. Tank equipment is built differently from pure damage builds.

STARTING THE Novice/SUPER PROGRESSION​

The Novice/Super Jumping system is designed to help new Tamers catch upsignificantly faster. After creating a new Tamer: Reach Tamer Level 2Once you reach Level 2, you will receive mail containing a Good starting Pack. Do not ignore this package. It contains several useful progression items, including tickets that can be exchanged for Digimon andaccessories intended to help you begin playing the diferent roles.

Important Note: super jumping is over However, those who still have the Jumping Mark are still able to complete the quests,

MAJOR GOAL​

LEVEL120​

After establishing your Jumping progression, work on leveling your Tamer and Digimon. A strong early target is: Tamer Level 120 Digimon Level 120 If you reach around Level 100 and leveling begins slowing down, look for other players farming on the samemap. Joining a farming party can significantly improve your leveling speed.

Level 120 Digivice Dailies After reaching Level 120, you will have access to two daily missions through your Digivice that can be usedto help boost Digimon to Level 120. ⚠️ Remember to switch to the Digimon you want to level BEFORE claiming the reward.

UNLOCK MAP​

New players should not spend all their time farming one area. Progress through the main missions and unlock your maps. Map progression is important because later areas contain: Dungeons Tours Questlines NPC Exchanges Progression Materials Gear Upgrades useful progression route to keep working through is: File Island → Server Continent → Tokyo → Spiral Mountain You should also progress through Shinjuku → Digital Area → Forest Area, because Forest Area is important for another useful beginner activity explained later in this guide.

DUNGEON PROGRESSION​

After finishing the important Jumping progression and establishing your Digimon, role and basic equipment, you can begin moving deeper into DMW's dungeon progression. The available DMW dungeon guides cover progression content including: Vajra Vika Chaotic Burning Final MDG PDG Mugen Pied Pillars APO Odaiba Park Extreme

Do not interpret this simply as: “I unlocked it, therefore I am ready for it.”

Before progressing into harder content, improve your Digimon, cloning, Deck, Seals, accessories and role-specific equipment. Mechanics also become increasingly important. A weaker player who understands the dungeon can often contribute more effectively than a stronger playerwho ignores mechanics.
