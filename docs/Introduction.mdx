## UNDERSTANDING DMW ROLES

One of the most important differences a new player should understand is that Digimon in DMW can fill
different combat roles.
The three main roles you should know are:

### Auto Attack DPS

AA builds focus primarily on dealing damage through normal attacks.
Your Digimon choice, accessories, Vice and other equipment should eventually be optimized around AA
performance.

### Skill DPS

SK builds focus primarily on dealing damage through Digimon skills.
SK players therefore use a different equipment setup from AA players as they progress.

### Tank

Tank builds focus on surviving encounters and handling the defensive side of party content.
Tank equipment is built differently from pure damage builds.

## MAJOR GOAL 

### LEVEL120

After establishing your Jumping progression, work on leveling your Tamer and Digimon.
A strong early target is:
Tamer Level 120
Digimon Level 120
If you reach around Level 100 and leveling begins slowing down, look for other players farming on the samemap.
Joining a farming party can significantly improve your leveling speed.

Level 120 Digivice Dailies
After reaching Level 120, you will have access to two daily missions through your Digivice that can be usedto help boost Digimon to Level 120.
⚠️ Remember to switch to the Digimon you want to level BEFORE claiming the reward.

### UNLOCK MAP 

New players should not spend all their time farming one area.
Progress through the main missions and unlock your maps.
Map progression is important because later areas contain:
