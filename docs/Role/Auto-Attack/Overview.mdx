# Auto Attack DPS / AA

> AA builds focus primarily on dealing damage through normal attacks.
Your Digimon choice, accessories, Vice and other equipment should eventually be optimized around AA
performance.

---

## Strengths

- High sustained DPS
- Easy to play
- Low DS consumption
- Excellent for long boss fights
- Great for farming

---

## Weaknesses

- Less burst damage
- Depends heavily on Attack Speed
- Requires strong accessories and seals

---

## Recommended For

- New players
- Farming
- Raid bosses
- Daily content
- Players who prefer simple rotations

---

## Core Seals Priority

1. Hit Rate (HT)
2. Attack (AT)
3. Health Point (HP)
4. Critical Rate (CT)
5. Skill (SK)
6. Digi-Soul (DS)
